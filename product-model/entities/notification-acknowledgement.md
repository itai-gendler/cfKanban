# Notification acknowledgement

Explicit confirmation by an identity of another publisher's notice. Reading a notice does not confirm it.

## Information kept

- **Notification** — The notification of this notification acknowledgement, retained or disclosed with the object.
- **Principal** — The principal of this notification acknowledgement, retained or disclosed with the object.
- **Acknowledged at** — The acknowledged at of this notification acknowledgement, retained or disclosed with the object.
